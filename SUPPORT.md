# Support

Stone is a self-hosted public-preview candidate, not a hosted service or production-certified
product. Support is best effort and no response time, uptime, migration, binary, or compatibility
guarantee is provided.

Use GitHub issues for reproducible Stone defects, documentation gaps, and narrowly scoped feature
proposals after searching existing issues. Include the commit/version, platform, expected/actual
behavior, minimal synthetic reproduction, and validation output with credentials removed.

Do not use issues for:

- vulnerabilities or anything containing secrets/private data (follow `SECURITY.md`);
- Firebase, GitHub, Apple, EAS, or cloud-provider account administration;
- recovery of lost credentials or unsupported history rewrites;
- general React Native/Tauri/Firebase support;
- public iOS binaries, a maintainer backend, or unimplemented roadmap promises.

Self-hosters are responsible for their infrastructure costs, rules, backups, identifiers, signing,
updates, and credential rotation. Physical-device support is limited to the validation evidence
published for the exact preview candidate.
