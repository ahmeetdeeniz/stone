# Code of conduct

## Our standards

Contributors and maintainers are expected to be respectful, patient, constructive, and welcoming.
Disagreement should address ideas and evidence, not people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory
language, sexualized attention, doxxing, and publishing another person's private data are not
acceptable.

## Scope

This applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, review, and other spaces where a
person represents the Stone project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through the repository's maintainer contact once the owner publishes a
safe private channel. No public contact is currently designated. Do not include sensitive personal
information in a public issue. The owner must establish that route before opening broad community
participation.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
behavior threatens safety or constructive collaboration. Enforcement should be proportionate,
documented privately where appropriate, and protect reporter confidentiality.
